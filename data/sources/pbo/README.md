# Australian Government total expenses, 2025-26

- **File:** `mtbo_2025-26_total_expenses.csv` — one row: total general government expenses for 2025-26, in A$ billion
  and as a percentage of GDP, as printed in the source table.
- **Publisher:** Parliamentary Budget Office (PBO), *2025-26 Medium-term budget outlook: Beyond the Budget*, Table B-2
  (Comparison of expense programs): total expenses A$785.7 billion, 27.3% of GDP.
  https://www.pbo.gov.au/publications-and-data/publications/2025-26-Medium-term-budget-outlook
- **Licence:** PBO publications are released under CC BY 4.0 (Commonwealth of Australia).
- **How it was obtained:** read from the PBO web page on 26/10/2026 and copied digit for digit; the build environment
  could not download files from pbo.gov.au.
- **Used for:** only Calculator K's "share of federal spending" = (cost as % of GDP) ÷ 27.3%. Using the
  percentage-of-GDP form keeps US-dollar costs and Australian-dollar budgets comparable without an exchange rate.
